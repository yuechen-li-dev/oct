# Prometheus M35b final historical shader replacement

Status: **COMPLETE — success** (2026-07-12)

M35b promotes the final two original Prometheus SPIR-V kernels to ordinary production SDSL-V assets. All five original historical kernels are now production SDSL-V-owned. IDs 13 (`sgemm-packed4`) and 14 (`sgemm-fp16-storage-fp32-accum`) are unchanged, as are policy gates, descriptor bindings, 12-byte `{m,n,k}` push constants, 8x8x1 workgroups, and 1x1 footprints.

The M34b entry-point policy is retained: registry and Vulkan pipeline creation use generated SDSL-V entry names, not historical `main`. Packed4 retains row-packed A / column-packed B `float4` buffers and `Dot`. FP16 retains packed `u32` storage, low-bit `.x` / high-bit `.y` ordering, and FP32 accumulation via `Unpack<F16x2>`. Neither source contains inline HLSL.

`generate_sdslv_shaders.ps1` remains the single manifest-driven generation path. The generated bytes exactly match M35a candidates. Historical originals remain under `DevelopmentReport/artifacts/SDSL_V_ORIGINAL_SPIRV_REWRITE/original/`; the M34a audit harness now loads those archive files, preventing production headers from serving as rollback evidence.

Windows native rebuild, manifest parity, `spirv-val`, production validation setup, and the archived-original nine-workload audit passed. Validation reported requested=true, layer available/enabled, debug-utils active, warnings=0, errors=0, and no device loss. Bounded 127x131x129 timing was Packed4 original/generated `9312/9344/9440` vs `7264/7296/7392` ns and FP16 `11360/11392/11584` vs `10752/10912/11072` ns (min/median/max).

Rollback restores the ID 13/14 registry and manifest records to `main` and historical source references, restores historical headers from the retained archive if needed, then reruns generation, `spirv-val`, manifest parity, native build, policy tests, and the audit matrix.
